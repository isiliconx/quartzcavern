SiO₂  ·  Hydrothermal pocket
FIELD NOTE   01 / α-QUARTZ

Inside a silica pocket

Clear terminations rise from a cloudy, iron-stained matrix. Look across a broad prism face for fine growth steps, or through a tip to see reflected rock.

This is an imagined vug. The chamber and accelerated deposition are artistic reconstructions. The pool and droplets move on a separate, ordinary timescale.

Drag or use arrow keys to orbit. Scroll, pinch, or press + / − to zoom. Glide returns after 4 seconds. Space pauses time and glide; H hides the labels. R restores the opening view.
